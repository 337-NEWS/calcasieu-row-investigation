All acquisitions of immovable property interests from January 1, 2022 through the date of this request**, including but not limited to: fee acquisitions, rights-of-way, temporary construction servitudes, permanent servitudes, drainage servitudes, utility servitudes, and any other real-property interests acquired for public projects. For each acquisition, please include:

- a. Project name and project number
- b. Property owner / payee name

- e. Acquisition or payment amount
- f. Appraisal or valuation relied upon

- h. Date the acquisition was accepted / completed
- i. Source of funding
- j. Right-of-way maps or acquisition plats
- k. Expropriation proceedings, if any (petition, judgment, deposits)
