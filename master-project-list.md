## Key facts for the investigation

- The 2026 budget letter explicitly lists **right-of-way acquisitions** as a transportation capital cost category — ROW spending is in the budget, which supports the records request.
- 2026 budget total: $379,623,652 (+7.96% vs 2025 amended). Capital projects ~$180.7M.
- Special service districts (Police Jury is governing board): Waterworks Districts 2 (Ward 4), 5 (Wards 3&8), 8 (Wards 3&8), 12 (Ward 3); Sewerage Districts 8 (Ward 4), 11 (Wards 3&8); Ward 3 Fire Protection District 2. Acquisition streams beyond the Police Jury itself will run through these.
