## Classification
- **PRIMARY SOURCE** — original government, legal, financial, property, meeting, correspondence, or database record.
- **SECONDARY SOURCE** — outside reporting or analysis.
- **REPORTER NOTES** — working research or analysis that is not itself evidence.

Where available, preserve the original source URL and retrieval date. Important archived files may also have a SHA-256 hash recorded.
