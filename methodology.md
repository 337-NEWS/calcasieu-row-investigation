# Methodology

This archive lets readers inspect the documentary basis of 337.NEWS reporting on Calcasieu Parish right-of-way acquisitions and related matters.

## Evidence standards
Claims in published reporting should be tied to identifiable source material whenever practicable. Primary records are preferred over summaries or secondary reporting. A document's presence here establishes only that it is relevant to the research; it does not establish misconduct or validate every statement it contains.

## Document handling
For preserved source documents, record provenance and retrieval date where practicable. Important original files may be accompanied by a SHA-256 hash. Documents should not be altered substantively. Material redactions, conversions, compression, or other processing should be disclosed.

## Privacy and redaction
Before publication, records should be reviewed for unrelated personal information whose mass republication is unnecessary to the public-interest purpose of the investigation, including sensitive identifiers, account information, signatures, and personal contact information.

## Working notes
Reporter notes may contain questions, leads, tentative connections, or hypotheses. They are not findings and should be clearly labeled **REPORTER NOTES**.

## Corrections
Material errors should be corrected transparently. Substantive corrections should be described in `corrections.md`, with Git history preserving the change.

## Published reporting
This repository is an evidence archive, not a substitute for finished journalism. Published 337.NEWS articles provide context, responses from subjects, and reported conclusions.
